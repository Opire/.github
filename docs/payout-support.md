# Bounty Payout Support

## Mainland China Individual Developers

As of this update, Opire does not currently support bounty payouts via Stripe Connect to individual developers residing in mainland China.

1. **Stripe Connect availability:** Stripe Express connected accounts and direct bank settlement are not available for individual recipients in mainland China.
2. **Alternative payout options:** There is currently no other officially supported payout method for individual developers in mainland China.
3. **Restrictions to confirm before contributing:** Identity verification, tax documentation, payout thresholds, and supported-country requirements are determined by our payment provider and may change. Please contact Opire support before starting bounty work if you are located in mainland China.

If Stripe expands support or Opire adds another payout method, this document will be updated.
